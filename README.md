# Wellness Products Market Analysis + Brands' TikTok Posts Review + Influencer Proposal

## General Supplement Market Analysis

#### Objective and Scope of Analysis

The goal of this analysis is to understand the overall competitive landscape of supplement products that offer similar benefits to Aussiesupps’ five core items. The scope includes top-performing products across three major platforms used in Thailand: Lazada, Shopee, and TikTok Shop.

---

#### Data Collection and Preprocessing

For each Aussiesupps product, I manually collected 30 competitor items, 10 from each platform resulting in a total of 150 products. I used the search bar on Lazada, Shopee, and TikTok Shop to filter and select best-selling products that matched the benefits of Aussiesupps’ items.

The data fields we collected included: Platform, Brand Name, Product Full Name, Manufacturing Country, Category, Effect, Price (THB), Package Size, Packaging Form, Sales Volume, etc. 

---

#### Analytical Tools and Calculated Metrics

Once the data was cleaned, I imported it into Power BI to build interactive dashboards and visualizations. These dashboards allowed me to explore product performance across platforms and identify key trends.

Key metrics analyzed included:
- Top 5 best-selling products per Aussiesupps item breakdown

## Premium Supplement Market Analysis

### Overview

After presenting the general supplement market analysis, my supervisor asked for a deeper look into the premium segment. To do this, I used the same dataset from the general analysis and began by defining three price tiers: A Tier, B Tier, and C Tier. I then filtered the A Tier products in Excel and created separate sheets for each Aussiesupps product category, containing only premium competitors.

Once the data was organized, I imported it into Power BI and built visualizations showing total sales across e-commerce platforms, manufacturing country distribution, and platform-specific performance.

To support deeper insights into the premium supplement segment, I visualized key metrics using Power BI such as summary visualizations across all tiers. These visual insights helped set the stage for a focused premium supplement analysis.
